Where to Start Reading the Bible If You Don't Believe

You do not need to believe anything to read the Bible this way. You need the text, a word list and a few lessons.

The Bible is one story told many times. Every story has the same three parts: someone present, a name or identity that person takes on, and judges whose ruling follows it. Genesis 1 sets those parts out first, and the course follows them from story to story. Start with the three parts:

  1. YHVH (the LORD), "the existing One": the one who is present.
  2. I AM: the name or identity that one takes on.
  3. Elohim: the judges and rulers, whose ruling follows the identity and makes it real.

Why read them as yours? Because Genesis 1 makes man the one who is made in the judges' own image. So the three parts are you, aware right now, the identity you claim, and the judges inside you that make it real. See the Bible's court language.

This is not the usual reading, so do not take it on trust. The four steps below let you test it against the verse and Strong's.

You can check every step

  1. Read the verse in the Bible in Basic English, with the chapter around it.
  2. Look up each name and place in Strong's.
  3. Put the meanings in place of the titles and read it again.
  4. See whether the reading fits the passage. If it does not, say so.
