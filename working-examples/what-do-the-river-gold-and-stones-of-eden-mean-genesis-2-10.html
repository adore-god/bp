What Do the River, Gold and Stones of Eden Mean? Genesis 2:10 to Revelation 22:4

Genesis 2 describes the river of Eden with a short list: gold, bdellium and the onyx stone. Many readers take it as a map. This page follows the words instead. The same gold and the same stone come back on the priest (Exodus 28), in Eden again (Ezekiel 28) and at the end of the Bible (Revelation 21 and 22), and each time they are carrying a name. The question the page asks is where the name ends up.

The short answer: the river goes out of Pleasure and parts into four heads. The land of the first river holds good gold and the onyx stone. Later that same onyx is cut with the names of the twelve sons of Israel and set in gold, and a gold plate goes on the priest's brow. At the end of the Bible there is no more curse, the river runs clear through a street of gold, and the name is on the brows. The course reads the river as what runs through the reader's own head, and the gold and the stone as the ground where a name (an identity) is set. The Bible keeps showing where that name is placed: on the stone, over the heart, on the brow.

In six lines:

  1. A river goes out of Eden (Pleasure) and parts into four heads (Genesis 2:10).
  2. The first river goes round Havilah (Circle), where the gold is good and the onyx stone is (2:11-12).
  3. Exodus 28 uses that onyx for the names of the twelve sons of Israel, set in gold, carried on the shoulders and over the heart, with a gold plate on the brow (28:9-12, 21, 29, 36-38).
  4. Ezekiel 28 says Eden was clothed in those same stones, and the one who sat in the seat of the Judges ends as dust (28:2, 13, 18).
  5. Revelation 22 has the river clear as glass, no more curse, and the name on the brows (22:1-5).
  6. The river that parted into heads in Genesis 2 ends with the name on the brow in Revelation 22.

The course in short

  • The Bible is read as a courtroom inside the reader (Lesson 1). The Judges (Elohim) rule on the identity the reader holds, and the Existing One, the reader's own present awareness, holds it as I AM.
  • Genesis 1 and 2 are the scene of pleasure the reader is trying to get back to (Lesson 5).
  • Everything comes back after its kind (Lesson 7): what is filed is what is enforced.
  • To leave one state and join another is the rule of Genesis 2:24.
  • Names are identity codes (the names lesson). The story turns out according to the name and the place, so every name below is read for its meaning.

The meanings used

  • Eden (H5731): "pleasure". The garden is the scene of pleasure of Genesis 1 and 2.
  • Heads (H7218, rosh): "head". The BBE says "streams" in Genesis 2:10, and the Hebrew is the word for the head of a person.
  • Pishon (H6376): "dispersive" in Strong's, "increase" in the Blue Letter Bible outline. This page uses "Spreading".
  • Havilah (H2341): "circle". The land the first river goes round.
  • Gihon (H1521): "stream" in Strong's, "bursting forth" in the outline.
  • Cush (H3568): "black" in the Blue Letter Bible outline. Strong's says the name is probably of foreign origin.
  • Hiddekel (H2313), the BBE's Tigris: "rapid".
  • Asshur (H804), the BBE's Assyria: "a step". Strong's says apparently from H833, "successful".
  • Perath (H6578), the BBE's Euphrates: "rushing" in Strong's, "fruitfulness" in the outline.
  • Onyx (shoham, H7718): occurs in 11 verses: Genesis 2:12, Exodus 25:7, 28:9, 28:20, 35:9, 35:27, 39:6, 39:13, 1 Chronicles 29:2, Job 28:16 and Ezekiel 28:13. The BBE says "beryl" at Exodus 28:9, and the Hebrew there is the same word as the onyx of Genesis 2:12.
  • Bdellium (H916): occurs in two verses, Genesis 2:12 and Numbers 11:7, where the manna has its look.
  • Israel (H3478): "he will rule as God" in Strong's, "God prevails" in Brown-Driver-Briggs. This page uses "He-prevails".
  • Aaron (H175): of uncertain derivation in Strong's, "light bringer" in the Blue Letter Bible outline.
  • Tyre (H6865): "a rock".
  • The Lord (YHVH, H3068): the Existing One. God (Elohim in the Old Testament, theos in Revelation): the Judges.

How to read it, step by step

  1. The river leaves Pleasure and parts into heads. It waters the garden first, then it parts (Genesis 2:10). The word the BBE gives as "streams" is the word for a head. The water that fed the scene of pleasure ends in four heads.
  2. Each head goes round a ground. Spreading goes round Circle, where there is gold, and the gold is called good, which is the verdict the Judges give all through Genesis 1. The land also has bdellium and the onyx stone. Bursting-forth goes round Black, Rapid goes east of A-step, and Rushing is named last. All four river names are words of movement.
  3. The onyx is the stone that carries the names. It is the same Hebrew word at Genesis 2:12 and Exodus 28:9. Aaron, Light-bringer, takes two of these stones, cuts the names of the sons of He-prevails on them, six and six in birth order, and has them fixed in frames of gold (28:9-11). The stone and the gold of the river's land become the setting for names.
  4. The names are carried in three places. On the shoulders, as stones of memory (28:12). Over the heart, on the twelve jewels, one tribe's name cut on each (28:21, 29). And on the brow, on a gold plate that reads HOLY TO THE LORD, "at all times" (28:36-38). The name moves from the stone to the heart to the head.
  5. Eden wears the same stones, and the voice that sits in the Judges' seat ends as dust. Ezekiel 28:13 lists the stones of Eden's covering, and they are nine of the twelve on the breastplate. The one wearing them has said "I am a god" and "I am seated on the seat of God" (28:2), and the answer is "you are man and not God". The stones are the ground for the name. They do not make the wearer the Judge. The ending is dust (28:18).
  6. Revelation puts it all in one place. The river is clear as glass and comes out of the high seat of the Judges and of the Lamb, in the middle of the street (22:1-2). The town and its street are gold like clear glass, its wall is jasper, and its foundations are twelve stones, with the names of the twelve tribes on the gates and the names of the twelve apostles on the foundations (21:12-21). There is no more curse, and his name is on their brows (22:3-4). Genesis 2 describes the river by how it moves. Revelation 22 describes it by how it looks: clear.

What the names add

The four rivers are Spreading, Bursting-forth, Rapid and Rushing, and they go round Circle, Black and the east of A-step. That is a picture of water going everywhere and round every ground. In Revelation the river is one river, clear, in the middle of the street, and the name that was cut on stone in Exodus is on the brow.

Light-bringer is the one who carries the names. He carries them on the shoulders, over the heart and on the brow, always "before the Lord", the Existing One, to keep them in mind. The ruler of Rock is the one who wears the same stones and says "I am a god", and the ending is dust.

Bdellium is in only two verses. It is in the land of Circle in Genesis 2:12, and in Numbers 11:7 the manna has its look.

What it means for the reader

The river is what runs through your own head. In Genesis 2 it spreads, bursts and rushes round every ground. The gold and the stone are the ground where a name is set, and the Bible keeps asking where yours is placed. In Exodus the names are on stone, over the heart and on the brow. In Ezekiel the stones are there but the voice sits in the Judges' seat, and it ends as dust. In Revelation the curse is gone, the river is clear, and the name is on the brow. Read the next time you meet gold or stone in the Bible as the same question: which name is set here, and who is wearing it?

Remember: the river parts into heads in Genesis 2 and the name is on the brows in Revelation 22. Between them, the gold and the onyx of the river's land are the setting for the name.
