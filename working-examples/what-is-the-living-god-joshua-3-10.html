What Is the "Living God"? (Joshua 3:10)

And Joshua said, By this you will see that the living God is among you, and that he will certainly send out from before you the Canaanite and the Hittite and the Hivite and the Perizzite and the Girgashite and the Amorite and the Jebusite. — Joshua 3:10 (BBE)

The Hebrew behind "the living God" is El chay: El (H410), "God", and chay (H2416), "living". Chay is the plain word for alive. Strong's and BDB give it for living things, for green plants, and for fresh flowing water. Those are the things Genesis 1 builds the scene from, so "living" is a Genesis word before it is anything else.

The ground. The speech is made at the Jordan. Jordan (H3383) means "a descender", from the verb "go down" (H3381). Five verses earlier Joshua tells the people, "Make yourselves holy" (Joshua 3:5, BBE). The order is: set apart first, then see the living God among you. See What does holy mean?

The same word in the first chapters. In Genesis 2:7 "man became a living soul" (BBE). In Genesis 3:20 the man names his wife:

And the man gave his wife the name of Eve because she was the mother of all who have life. — Genesis 3:20 (BBE)

Eve is Chavvah (H2332), "life-giver". BDB reads the reason in the verse as "mother of every chay", the same word as in Joshua 3:10. The first woman is named for it, so "living" is what the identity in Genesis carries from the start (names are codes).

"I am", not "I was".

I am the God of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob? God is not the God of the dead but of the living. — Matthew 22:32 (BBE)

Luke 20:37 says this line is from the story of the burning thorn-tree, where the name is "I am" (Lesson 2). In this reading, "God" is the judges and rulers (Lesson 3), and the judges are the court of the living: they rule for the identity that is alive now, the one held as "I am". An identity kept only as a memory or a proof from the past is the "dead" one.

Where the word lists differ.

  • El (H410): in Joshua 3:10 the Hebrew for "God" is El, not Elohim. This page reads "living" from chay and does not claim El is Elohim.
  • Eve (H2332): BDB also records a proposal that the name means "serpent". It gives "life" first, and Genesis 3:20 states the reason itself.
  • Matthew 22:32: the Greek word was not checked here, so no claim is built on it.

Remember: "living" is the Genesis word for what is green, flowing and alive, and Eve carries it in her name. The living God is the one present now.
