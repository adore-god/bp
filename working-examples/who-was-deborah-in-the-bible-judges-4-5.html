Who Was Deborah in the Bible? Judges 4 and 5

Deborah is the only woman named as a judge in the book of Judges, and her story is told twice: once as an account (Judges 4) and once as a song (Judges 5). Many readers take it as a war story. This page follows the words instead: her name, the names around her, and where the story puts the verdict. The question it asks is who speaks and who acts.

The short answer: the book of Judges runs one loop again and again. Israel takes up an identity (a judge), it holds the scene for as long as it lives, then it dies and the scene falls back (Judges 2:16-19). Judges 4 opens with that fall. United (Ehud) is dead, and the land is ruled by Intelligent (Jabin), king of Humbled (Canaan), from Village (Hazor), with Battle-array (Sisera) and nine hundred iron war-carriages. Bee (Deborah, a name from the word for speak) sits as judge, the wife of Torches, under a tree that carries her name, and she speaks the order. Lightning (Barak) will only go if she goes with him, which is the rule of Genesis 2:24: go away from the old state and be joined to the wife, the identity. The win goes to a woman, Mountain-goat (Jael), who ends Battle-array in her tent. At the end of the chapter the Judges (Elohim) humble the king of the Humbled, and Judges 5 sings it. The course reads Deborah as the identity that holds the verdict and speaks it, and the story shows what that looks like from the first word to the last.

In six lines:

  1. In Judges the Existing One gives judges as saviours, each holds the scene while it lives, and when the judge is dead Israel goes back (Judges 2:16-19).
  2. Judges 4:1: United is dead, Israel does evil again, and is given up into the hand of Intelligent, king of Humbled, for twenty years.
  3. Bee, a woman prophet and the wife of Torches, is judge, sitting under the palm-tree of Bee between Height and House-of-God in the Double-fruit hill-country (4:4-5).
  4. She sends for Lightning, who will go only if she goes with him. She goes, and says the honour will not be his, because Battle-array will be given into the hands of a woman (4:6-9).
  5. Lightning goes down from Mount Broken-region, Battle-array leaves his iron war-carriage and runs on foot to the tent of Mountain-goat, and is ended there (4:14-22).
  6. The Judges humble Intelligent, king of Humbled, Bee is told to give a song, and the land has peace for forty years (4:23-24, 5:12, 5:31).

The course in short

  • The Bible is read as a courtroom inside the reader (Lesson 1). The Judges (Elohim, Lesson 3) rule on the identity the reader holds, and the Existing One, the reader's own present awareness, holds it as I AM.
  • Genesis 1 and 2 are the scene of pleasure the reader is trying to get back to (Lesson 5). The lights of day four mark when something is due.
  • Everything comes back after its kind (Lesson 7): what is filed is what is enforced.
  • Man is the one who claims an identity (Lesson 6). The wife is the identity he takes on, and to take it on he leaves the old state and is joined to her (Genesis 2:24). Male and female are a story device, not a statement about gender, and both are in the reader.
  • Names are identity codes (the names lesson). The story turns out according to the name and the place, so every name below is read for its meaning.

The book Deborah is in

Judges comes after Joshua. Israel is in the land, but Judges 1 keeps saying the same thing: tribe after tribe did not drive the Canaanites out, and the Canaanites went on living among them (1:27-34). Judges 2:1-3 gives the verdict, with the order that was not kept: make no agreement with the people of this land. Then comes a generation with no knowledge of the Existing One (2:10), and the loop starts: they do evil, they are given up into the hands of their haters, they cry out, the Existing One gives them a judge as saviour, and the judge holds the scene all the days of the judge. When the judge is dead they go back (2:11-19). Judges 3:5-6 says why the loop begins: they lived among the old nations, took their daughters as wives, and served their gods. In this reading the wife is the identity taken on, so Israel keeps being joined to identities from the old ground, which is Genesis 2:24 run the wrong way. The book ends with no king and every man doing what seemed right to him (21:25), each man his own judge. The book is named for the judges, from the Hebrew shaphat, to pronounce sentence for or against.

The meanings used

  • Judge, judges (shaphat, H8199): to pronounce sentence for or against, to vindicate or punish, to govern. Brown-Driver-Briggs gives Genesis 18:25, "the Judge of all the earth", as the use of God only, and Judges 2:16 and 2:18 for the judges the Existing One sets over Israel.
  • Israel (H3478): "he will rule as God" in Strong's, "God prevails" in Brown-Driver-Briggs. This page uses "He-prevails".
  • Ehud (H164): "united".
  • Jabin (H2985): "intelligent", from H995. Brown-Driver-Briggs: "one who is intelligent, discerning".
  • Canaan (H3667): "humiliated", from H3665, the verb that Judges 4:23 uses for the Judges "subduing" Jabin. The Blue Letter Bible outline gives "lowland". This page uses "Humbled".
  • Hazor (H2674): "village", a collective form of H2691. The outline gives "castle". This page uses "Village".
  • Sisera (H5516): Strong's says of uncertain derivation. The outline gives "battle array". This page uses "Battle-array".
  • Harosheth of the Gentiles (H2800, H1471): H2800 is the same as H2799, "carving". Brown-Driver-Briggs gives "woodland". The Gentiles are the nations. This page uses "Carving-of-the-Gentiles".
  • Deborah (H1683): "bee", the same as H1682, which Strong's gives as from H1696, the root meaning to speak. The Hebrew for "give a song" at Judges 5:12 is that same speaking root (H1696).
  • Lapidoth (H3941): "torches", the plural of H3940, a torch.
  • Woman prophet (H5031): a prophetess or inspired woman, by implication a poetess.
  • Ramah (H7414): the same as H7413, a height. Brown-Driver-Briggs: "the Height".
  • Beth-el (H1008): "house of God", from H1004 and H410.
  • Ephraim (H669): "double fruit". Genesis 41:52 gives the reason: made fruitful.
  • Barak (H1301): "lightning", the same as H1300.
  • Abinoam (H42): "father of pleasantness (i.e. gracious)", from H1 and H5278. Brown-Driver-Briggs: "my father is delight".
  • Kedesh (H6943): "a sanctum", from H6942. The outline gives "holy place".
  • Naphtali (H5321): "my wrestling", from H6617. Genesis 30:8 gives the reason: a great fight, and overcome.
  • Zebulun (H2074): "habitation", from H2082. The outline gives "exalted".
  • Tabor (H8396): "broken region". The outline gives "mound".
  • Kishon (H7028): "winding", from H6983.
  • Heber (H2268): "community". The outline gives "comrade".
  • Kenite (H7017): a patronymic from H7014, Cain (Qayin). In Judges 4:11 the Hebrew for "the Kenites" that Heber separated himself from is H7014 itself.
  • Jael (H3278): the same as H3277, the ibex or mountain goat.
  • Sold (makar, H4376): "sell". At Judges 2:14, 3:8, 4:2, 4:9 and 10:7 the Hebrew is the same word, where the BBE says "gave up" or "give".
  • The Lord (YHVH, H3068): the Existing One. God (Elohim, H430): the Judges. The Lord, the God of Israel (Judges 4:6): the Existing One, the Judges of He-prevails.

How to read it, step by step

  1. The book is a loop. Israel is joined to the old ground (3:6), a judge is given and holds the scene all the days of the judge (2:18), the judge dies, and the scene falls back after its kind (2:19). Each judge is an identity taken up for a time, not a person to admire. The Judges (Elohim) are the court that holds the verdict, and the judges of the book are the identities that court enforces while they live.
  2. Judges 4 opens with the fall. "When United was dead" is the whole cause. The last identity has ended, and the old kind comes back. The Existing One "gave them up", in Hebrew sold them, into the hand of Intelligent, king of Humbled, ruling from Village. The old state is run by the reasoning mind on a lowered ground, shut in its village. Its captain is Battle-array, who lives in Carving-of-the-Gentiles, and its machinery is iron: nine hundred iron war-carriages, twenty years of hard pressure. Israel (He-prevails) prays, which is the cry that always starts the next judge.
  3. Bee is the bench. She is a woman prophet, an inspired woman and a poetess, and she is the wife of Torches, so the identity is joined to light. She is judge, the same word as "the Judge of all the earth" in Genesis 18:25, to pronounce sentence for or against. Her seat is under a palm-tree that carries her name, and a tree is an identity claim planted in the ground (Lesson 7). It stands between Height and House-of-God in the hill-country of Double-fruit, and the children of He-prevails come up to her to be judged. The name Bee comes from the word to speak, so the verdict is spoken. The only other Deborah in the Bible is Rebekah's nurse, buried below Beth-el under an oak (Genesis 35:8), so the name is already tied to House-of-God in Genesis.
  4. She speaks the order, and he will not go alone. She sends for Lightning, the son of Father-of-pleasantness, from Holy-place-of-My-wrestling, and gives the Existing One's order: Mount Broken-region, ten thousand from My-wrestling and Habitation, and Battle-array drawn to the river Winding. Lightning's answer is Genesis 2:24: if you will go with me I will go, and if not, I will not. The man does not go without the wife. She goes, and says the honour will not be his, because Battle-array will be given into the hands of a woman. In Hebrew that is the same word as 4:2: Israel was sold into the hand of a king, and now the captain is sold into the hand of a woman.
  5. "Today." Bee says "today" (4:14), and the stars from heaven fight from their highways against Battle-array (5:20). The lights of day four mark when something is due, and here they stand on the side of the verdict. Lightning comes down from Mount Broken-region, the Existing One sends fear, and Battle-array gets down from his iron war-carriage and runs on foot. All his army falls and not a man gets away (4:16), and the river Winding takes them away (5:21). The iron machine is left behind.
  6. The tent of peace. Battle-array runs to the tent of Mountain-goat, the wife of Community the one of Cain. The word Kenite comes from the name of Cain (Lesson 17), the line built from the cursed ground. There was "peace" between Intelligent, king of Village, and Community's house, an agreement with the old ruler, the very thing Judges 2:2 said not to make. It is the place the captain thinks is safe, and Mountain-goat says "without fear". He ends there with his head through to the earth. The woman who speaks the verdict and the woman who carries it out are the identity at both ends. Lightning arrives after, and Mountain-goat shows him the man he was looking for (4:22). The one who claims is given the sight of it, not the honour.
  7. The Judges finish it. Through the chapter the Existing One does the giving, sending and going out. At 4:23 it is "God" (Elohim) alone, the Judges, who overcame Intelligent, king of Humbled. The verb is the one the name Canaan comes from, so the Judges humble the king of the Humbled. The name acts itself out, after its kind.
  8. The song and what follows. Bee is told to "give a song", the speaking word her name comes from, and she comes up "as a mother in He-prevails". The land has peace for forty years (5:31). The book never says when Deborah ends. The next chapter opens with Israel doing evil again and being given up to a new ruler (6:1), which is the loop of 2:19 running once more.

What the names add

Bee is the wife of Torches, and Lightning is the one who goes down. Both are light words around the one who speaks. Against them stand Intelligent, Humbled, Village, Battle-array and iron: a reasoning mind on a lowered ground, shut in and hardened. Mountain-goat, of Community the one of Cain, ends it in a tent of peace, and the Judges close it with the verb behind the king's own name.

What it means for the reader

Judges describes a loop in you: an identity is held, the scene is good while it lives, it ends, and the old ruler comes back. Deborah's page shows what holding the identity with the verdict looks like. The verdict is spoken, not argued. The one who claims does not go on his own, he goes with the identity he is joined to. The glory belongs to the identity, not to the claimant. And the old state's captain is ended in the one place he trusts, the peace made with him. Read the next judge in the book as the same question: which identity is held, and what happens when it ends?

Remember: in Judges the held identity ends and the old kind returns. In Judges 4 the identity that holds the verdict is a woman named Bee, who speaks, and the one who claims goes with her.
