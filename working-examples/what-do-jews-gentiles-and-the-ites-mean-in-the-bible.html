What Do 'Jew', 'Gentile' and the '-ites' Mean in the Bible?

People ask who the Jews, the Gentiles and the Hittites, Amorites and Jebusites are. In this course they are words in the story, and each word tells you what it means. Jew means "praise". Gentile means "nation". Each -ite means "belonging to" the name in front of it. This page is not about any people alive today. It is about what the words do in the plot. Read the verses first, then read them again with the meanings put in. One reminder: in the BBE, "God" stands for Elohim, which is plural, so wherever you see "God", read the Judges.

The short answer: a Jew is one who belongs to Praise, because the word is built from the name Judah. A Gentile is a nation, a mass of people under one name, and Israel is called one too. An -ite is one who belongs to a name, like the Hittite, who belongs to Terror. Read from your side, the Praised-line is the identity you hold when you give praise. The nations are the crowd of voices in you. Each -ite is a voice that belongs to the state its name describes (About this reading).

The meanings used:

  • Judah (H3063) is "praised", and the verse says why: "I will give praise". Jew (H3064) is built from Judah, and Strong's calls a Jew a "Jehudite". The page reads it as "Praised-line".
  • Gentile / nation (H1471, goy) is "a nation", from a word for massing. The page reads it as "massed people".
  • God (Elohim, H430) is plural: the judges and rulers. The Lord (YHVH, H3068) is "the Existing One", you, aware right now (Lesson 3).
  • Israel (H3478) is "God prevails" (Blue Letter Bible) or "he will rule as God" (Strong's). The page reads it as "Prevails".
  • Moab sounds like "from father" and Ben-ammi is "son of my people". Edom is "red".
  • Canaanite (H3669): Canaan (H3667) is "humiliated". Hittite (H2850): Heth (H2845) is "terror". Amorite (H567) is "a sayer". Perizzite (H6522) is "inhabitant of the open country". Hivite (H2340) is "a villager". Jebusite (H2983): Jebus (H2982) is "trodden, i.e. threshing-place".

How to read it, step by step

  1. Jew is a praise word. Leah gives a son the name Judah in the very sentence where she says she will give praise (Genesis 29:35). Jew comes from that name. So the Praised-line is the line that carries praise as its nature (Lesson 9).
  2. You can become one. In Esther, after the second writing goes out, a great number of the people of the land "became Jews" (Esther 8:17). In Zechariah, ten men from all the nations grab the skirt of a Jew and say "God is with you" (Zechariah 8:23), which reads as the Judges are with you. And Romans 2 says the true Jew is the one who is so inwardly, whose praise is from God, the Judges. So Jew is the identity you hold, not what shows on the outside.
  3. Gentile just means nation, and Israel is one. The Hebrew word is goy, a mass of people under one name. Israel itself is called "a holy nation" (Exodus 19:6), and that word is goy. The word goy is used long before the word Jew, which first turns up in 2 Kings 16:6, and most of the places it is used are in Esther. So a nation is the mass of voices, and a holy nation is the mass gathered under one claim, with the Judges keeping it together.
  4. The -ite means belonging to. Jew, Hittite and Amorite are the same kind of word: one who belongs to a name. The only difference is the name in front. So read the name. The Hittite belongs to Terror, the Amorite belongs to Sayer, and the Jew belongs to Praise (Lesson 7, after its kind).
  5. The story shows the name. Moab, Ammon and Edom are named in the verse itself. For the others, look at what happens around them (the table below).
  6. The land is full of named states, and the story is about what you join. Genesis 15:19-21 gives the land to Abram's seed with ten named kinds already living in it. Then in Judges 3:5-6, Prevails lives among the Humiliated, the sons of Terror, the Sayers, the Open-country-dwellers, the Villagers and the men of the Threshing-place. It takes their daughters as wives and becomes servant to their Judges. Taking a wife is joining an identity (Lesson 8), and the Judges rule what you join.

What the stories show about each name

NameMeaningThe story
MoabFrom-fatherThe older daughter has a son and gives him the name Moab (Genesis 19:37). His mother is the daughter of the man he is named from, so the name holds both sides of one house.
AmmonSon-of-my-peopleThe younger daughter names her son Ben-ammi (Genesis 19:38). One son is named for the father and the other for the people.
EdomRedEsau asks for "that red soup" and "for this reason he was named Edom" (Genesis 25:30). He is named from what he is reaching for (Edom, Esau and Seir).
KeniteOne of Possession (also "smiths")Tubal-cain is "the father of every maker of cutting instruments of brass and iron" (Genesis 4:22). His name has Cain in it, so both meanings sit in Cain's own line (Lesson 17).
JebusiteThreshing-place"The angel of the Lord was by the grain-floor of Araunah the Jebusite" (2 Samuel 24:16). The man is the Jebusite and the place is the threshing-floor.
CanaaniteHumiliatedNoah curses Canaan to be the lowest of servants to his brothers (Genesis 9:25). The name is the curse acted out.
PerizziteOpen-country-dwellersAbram's and Lot's herdsmen argue, and the Canaanites and Perizzites are "still living in the land" (Genesis 13:7). The scene is herds and grazing, open country, like the Genesis scene before anything is labelled.

What changed from Genesis 1

Lesson 5 says every story is the Genesis 1 picture, changed. In Genesis 1 the scene is built and the man rules the creatures. Here the words are the names of what fills the scene.

Remember: read the name in front of the ending. Jew is Praise, a nation is a mass of people under one name, and each -ite belongs to the state its name describes. Wherever the BBE says "God", read the Judges. The text says people can become Jews, and that the true one is the inward one, so these are words for what an identity holds and what it joins.

Words checked

  • All verses are the BBE, except Genesis 9:25, 36:11 and Numbers 32:12, which were read in other translations. Meanings are from Strong's and Blue Letter Bible.
  • I read all 76 places where "Jew" (H3064) is used. It first appears in 2 Kings 16:6, and most of the places are in Esther.
  • Where two meanings exist, the page picks one: Canaan is "humiliated" (Strong's) or "lowland" (Blue Letter Bible), Amorite is "a sayer" or "mountaineer", Israel is "God prevails" or "he will rule as God", and Perizzite and Hivite both point to country or villages, so the page splits them.
  • H7014 is the number your Cain page gives for Cain, and Strong's gives the Kenite as "from H7014", so the Kenite is the one of Possession.
  • Not read yet: the stories for the Hittite, Hivite, Amorite, Girgashite and Kadmonite. The Greek words for Jew and nation (Romans) were not pulled.
