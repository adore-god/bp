Man Is Made by the Court

And the Judges said, Let us make man in our image, like us: and let him have rule over the fish of the sea and over the birds of the air and over the cattle and over all the earth and over every living thing which goes flat on the earth. — Genesis 1:26 (BBE)

In this course "man" does not mean a kind of body. Man is the one the court makes. The "us" is the court of many voices deciding what exists, and the verse says what they do with that decision: "Let us make man in our image." The court rules, and man is what the ruling produces.

Man says nothing in Genesis 1. Every other part of the chapter is made when the court speaks: "Let there be light: and there was light." Man is made the same way, by the court's ruling, and in the court's own likeness of judgement. The identity is set by the ruling.

The verse then gives that identity rule over the scene of creature groups from days five and six. Once the ruling stands, the identity rules those groups. They do not rule it.

See the patriarchs: the name before the story.

Remember: man is made by the court's judgement. Judgement sets the identity, and he stands in it and rules what it puts under him.
