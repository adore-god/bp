The Genesis 1 Word List

The seven days of creation fix the words the whole Bible uses. When a later story mentions a sea, a field, a star or a bird, it is taking a word from this list.

And the earth was waste and without form; and it was dark on the face of the deep: and the Spirit of God was moving on the face of the waters. And God said, Let there be light: and there was light. — Genesis 1:2-3 (BBE)
DayWords you will meetWhat the group stands for
1darkness, deep, pit, lightThe state before a new identity is spoken, then the first word spoken
2arch (firmament), heaven, waters above and belowThe edge between where you are now and where you are heading
3dry land, ground, garden, field, wilderness, seed, tree, vineThe ground you stand on, and what grows from it
4sun, moon, stars, seasonsThe set times: when something is due
5sea creatures, great fish, birdsWhat holds you, and what carries messages
6land creatures, man, womanThe identity itself, and union
7rest, Sabbath, completionThe identity held as already done

A note on wording: the BBE says “solid arch” where most Bibles say “firmament”.

Remember: darkness comes before light, because both belong to day one. The court always goes down before it brings you out.
