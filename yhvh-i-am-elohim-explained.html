YHVH, I AM and Elohim: The Three Parts

Every story runs on three parts. Learn these and the rest is easy.

PartWhat it meansWhat it does
YHVH (the LORD)“The existing One” (Strong’s H3068). You, aware right nowOffers an identity
I AMThe identity you choose and claimThe ruling you ask for
ElohimThe judges and rulers: many inner voicesMake real the identity you hold most firmly

How it works: YHVH offers an I AM, and Elohim makes it real. Elohim is not one person. It is a team of voices that rule as one.

Example, Joseph: in the pit, Joseph is YHVH, aware of where he is right now. The identity of ruler is the I AM. The rules of reality that carry it out are Elohim. Joseph goes from the pit to the palace.

Read more: what YHVH, LORD and Elohim each mean and I AM, the verdict YHVH presents.

Remember: YHVH offers, I AM is what is offered, Elohim makes it real.
